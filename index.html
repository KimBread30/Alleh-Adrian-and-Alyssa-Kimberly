Our Story

Some love stories begin with grand gestures. Theirs started with a simple glance in the middle of a busy afternoon at WalterMart Lawas.

Among the crowd, Alleh noticed Alyssa. There was nothing planned about that moment, yet something about her caught his attention and lingered in his mind long after she was gone. Curious and determined, he asked a close friend if they happened to know who she was. That single question set off a chain of events that felt almost destined. Friends reached out to friends, connections were traced one by one, and after a careful search, they finally found Alyssa's Facebook profile.

On October 18, 2024, Alleh sent the message that would quietly change both of their lives.

What started as a simple conversation soon became something they looked forward to every day. Good morning messages turned into late-night talks. Random updates became reasons to smile. The more they got to know each other, the more they discovered a comfort that felt natural—as if they had known each other for much longer than they actually had.

For a long time, they chose to keep their relationship lowkey, protecting the beautiful connection they were building away from the spotlight. In those quiet moments, they created countless memories together—shared laughter over the smallest things, conversations that lasted for hours, and the kind of happiness that made ordinary days feel special.

As their relationship grew, so did Alleh's intentions. His feelings were never just temporary; they were sincere and purposeful. Wanting to show the seriousness of his commitment, he visited Alyssa's home and formally introduced himself to her parents. With respect, honesty, and genuine care, he earned their trust and proved that his love for Alyssa was something he intended to cherish for the long run.

What began as a passing glance in a crowded shopping center has become a story filled with unwavering love, countless smiles, and a bond strengthened by time. A moment of curiosity became a beautiful journey of two hearts finding their way to each other—one message, one conversation, and one unforgettable memory at a time.

And even now, whenever they look back on how it all started, they can't help but smile at the thought that sometimes, the most extraordinary love stories begin with the simplest hello.
